---
title: Product profiles
summary: Every company has a product profile; a workspace may have extra capabilities that its operator switched on.
---

Every company carries a product profile. Most workspaces use the default, and the core product described in these docs works the same everywhere.

A workspace may also have capabilities that its operator switched on. Where a page describes one of these, it says so.

If something on a page is not in your workspace, it is not switched on there. Ask whoever runs your workspace.

Source: `packages/db/src/schema/companies.ts`, `packages/shared/src/constants.ts`
