# AgentDash — Design Partner Walkthrough

**Tagline:** Hire a Chief of Staff agent. It answers to you.

**Status:** Self-hosted and open source today. A hosted environment is in design.

---

## The problem

When your workforce is agents, a to-do list is not enough. You need a control plane: who owns what, why it matters, what it costs, and what waits on a human.

## What AgentDash is

AgentDash is the control plane for an agent company. Every agent has an accountable human steward. You hire a Chief of Staff, direct it from Claude Code or Codex, and let it delegate across the rest of your agent workforce — with hires, spend, deletions, and outside contact gated on your decision.

Built on open-source [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip). AgentDash adds the steward relationship, the harness-side inbox (MCP), and human-in-the-loop controls.

## How a walkthrough works (45–60 min)

Bring one real thing you would hand a Chief of Staff. Together we will:

1. **Frame the company** — goal, who stewards what, what "done" looks like
2. **Run the CoS path** — interview → proposed team → your confirm (nothing risky ships without you)
3. **Direct from the harness** — Claude Code or Codex via MCP: propose → read-back → confirm
4. **Show the gates** — approvals bound to a revision, steward inbox, budget hard-stops, activity log
5. **Cost and next step** — what this run would cost, and whether a managed pilot or self-host install fits

## What you leave with

- A clear picture of how AgentDash would run *your* work
- A list of what waits on you vs what agents can do alone
- An honest readiness call: install yourself, join a limited managed pilot, or wait for hosted

## Honest boundaries

- Not a chatbot. Work lives in goals, issues, comments, and approvals.
- Not "start free / no credit card" self-serve cloud — that product is not shipping yet.
- We do not invent customer counts or pricing on the public site.
