## Email body

**Subject:** Walkthrough: AgentDash on your own work

Hi {{first_name}},

Most "agent" tools still leave you babysitting terminals and hoping nothing expensive or irreversible happens.

AgentDash is a control plane for an agent company: every agent has an accountable human steward. You hire a Chief of Staff, direct it from Claude Code or Codex, and anything risky — hires, spend, deletions, outside contact — waits on your decision.

It's self-hosted and open source today. Hosted cloud is still in design (we're not pretending otherwise).

I'd like 45–60 minutes on one real thing you'd hand a Chief of Staff. We'll walk through how AgentDash would run it, what would wait on you, and what it would cost — then you can decide: install yourself, join a limited managed pilot, or wait.

If that sounds useful, reply with a time that works, or email edward@agentdash.cloud.

— {{your_name}}

Repo: https://github.com/thetangstr/agentdash
