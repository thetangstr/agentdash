local synthetic company. They show guided exact-output criteria and a task
mapped to the approved responsibility before work starts. They are not a
before/after comparison, customer usage, a model benchmark or human acceptance.

in the criteria editor reflects that fixture's board role, not genuine human
review or calibrated human labels. Human calibration and real outcome
acceptance remain pending. Exact document matching does not measure semantic
quality or business value.
