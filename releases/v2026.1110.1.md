# v2026.1110.1

> Released: 2026-11-10.

Bulk changes are now for company owners and admins only. Members keep changing records one at a time, exactly as before. No migrations.

## What's new

- **Bulk changes are for owners and admins.** Selecting several records and changing them together now needs a company owner or admin. This covers every kind of bulk change: tasks on the Issues list, approvals in Decisions, agents on the Agents page, routines on the Routines page, and deleting several labels in Company Settings. Members no longer see the checkboxes or the bulk bar.
- **Members keep every single-record change.** A member changes a task, decides an approval, pauses an agent, archives or deletes a routine, and renames, recolors or deletes a label one at a time, exactly as before. Deleting one label asks the member to confirm first, then deletes it directly.

## For admins

- Nothing to turn on. If a member used bulk changes on v2026.1110.0, either make the change yourself or make that person an admin.
- An approval that only a member may decide, for example one waiting on the member who looks after the agent that asked, is now decided one at a time by that member.
- Batch changes from an admin's own Claude were already for owners and admins only. They are unchanged.

## Upgrade

- No migrations and no configuration changes on top of v2026.1110.0.
- Upgrading from v2026.1109.1 runs the same four migrations as v2026.1110.0, `0158` to `0161`: `0158` adds the batch tables; `0159` adds the ceiling templates table and five company columns (the three switches, the records-per-batch number and the ceiling for new agents); `0160` adds a column that lets a routine be deleted while its runs stay, and one column to the batch table; `0161` adds two indexes to the activity log.
- `0161` builds its indexes with a plain `CREATE INDEX`, so writes to the activity log, and with them most writes in the product, wait while each index builds. On a large installation, build both first in a quiet window with `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY IF NOT EXISTS "activity_log_company_actor_created_idx" ON "activity_log" ("company_id", "actor_id", "created_at");` and `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY IF NOT EXISTS "activity_log_company_action_created_idx" ON "activity_log" ("company_id", "action", "created_at");`. The migration then does nothing.
- A bulk change a member previewed on v2026.1110.0 and has not applied can no longer be applied. A bulk change running when the server restarts for the upgrade is interrupted, as on any restart: records being sent are marked unknown and the rest are not sent.

## Security

- On v2026.1110.0, any member could make bulk changes, limited to what they could change one record at a time. This release limits bulk changes to owners and admins. The role is checked when the change is previewed and again when it is applied, so someone who stops being an admin in between cannot apply it. It is checked again before each record, so a bulk change stops if the person stops being an owner or admin while it runs.
- A member who tries anyway is refused with "Bulk changes are for company owners and admins.", and the refusal is recorded like other refused requests.

## Breaking Changes

- Members (not owners or admins) now get 403 from `POST /api/companies/:companyId/batches/preview` and `POST /api/companies/:companyId/batches/:batchId/confirm`. The local operator and instance admins are treated as admins, as on other owner and admin routes. Listing, reading and cancelling a member's own earlier batches is unchanged.
