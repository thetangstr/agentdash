# v2026.1110.0

> Released: 2026-11-10.

Anyone can now change many records at once, with a preview of exactly what will happen, limited to what they could already change one record at a time. Owners and admins also get an audit page with a CSV export, ceiling templates, a choice of who takes over a leaving member's agents, and, if they turn it on, batch changes prepared by their own Claude that only they can confirm. Four migrations run on upgrade.

## What's new

- **Change many records at once, with a preview.** On the Issues list, select tasks and change their status, assignee, labels, project or priority together. In Decisions, approve or reject several of the approvals waiting on you, with one shared note. On the Agents page, people who can manage agents can pause or resume several agents, change their model, hand them to a new steward or apply a ceiling template. On the Routines page, archive or delete several routines. In Company Settings, delete several labels. Every batch shows each record's before and after first. Nothing changes until you apply it, and each record goes through the same permission check as changing it by hand, so a batch can never do something you could not do one at a time. Up to 200 records per batch.
- **Honest results, record by record.** After you apply a batch, each record is marked Changed; Changed, but not as previewed (with what it is now); Not changed (with the reason); Failed (nothing was changed); or Unknown (it was sent and no answer came back). An unknown record is never retried by itself. A record that changed after you previewed it is not touched.
- **An audit page for owners and admins.** See who did what in the company, filtered by person, action and dates, with a CSV export of up to 50,000 rows. Open it from Admin powers in Company Settings, or from Activity. It shows only the activity the same person could already see on the Activity page, and every export is itself recorded.
- **Ceiling templates.** Where agent ceilings are available, owners and admins can name a ceiling once in Company Settings, and people who can manage agents can apply it to many agents with a preview of what each agent gains and loses. A company can also choose a ceiling that every new agent starts with; agents that already exist keep theirs.
- **Choose who takes over when you remove a member.** The remove-member dialog lists the agents the person looks after and lets you pick a new steward for each one, one person for all of them, or none. A person can look after only one agent per company, and the dialog says so.
- **Routines can be deleted.** Deleting a routine removes it from the routine lists for good and turns its triggers off. Its past runs, the tasks they created and its history are kept.
- **Labels can be renamed and recolored**, in a new Labels card in Company Settings, where you can also delete one label or several. As before, any member can manage labels.
- **Batch changes from an admin's own Claude (off by default).** When a company turns this on, an owner or admin can ask their connected Claude to prepare a change to many records. Claude can only prepare it. The code that confirms it is shown only on an AgentDash review page the admin opens in their own browser; the admin reads the code back to Claude, or presses Apply here on that page. This has been tested against AgentDash's own test client and reviewed, but it has not yet been tried with Claude.ai itself.
- **Two optional read powers for admins (off by default).** An owner or admin can read the directives of any agent, where agent directives are available, and open the transcript of any agent's run when document access limits who reads it. Directive reads are recorded every time; transcript reads are recorded once per person and run every 10 minutes. Neither works through a Claude connection.

## For admins

- Company Settings has a new Admin powers section: three switches, all off by default (admins can read agent directives; admins can read run transcripts when document access is on; allow batch changes from an admin's own Claude), the most records per batch from Claude (1 to 200, default 50), the ceiling for new agents, and a link to the admin audit. Only owners and admins can change them.
- The bulk tools need no switch. Each person sees them where they could already make the change one record at a time. The audit page is for owners and admins only.
- To use batch changes from Claude, turn the switch on, then connect Claude again and tick "Batch changes as an admin" on the consent screen. It is never pre-checked, and a connection made before this release gets it only by connecting again. Turning the switch off refuses further prepares and confirms and stops a running Claude batch before its next record; it does not remove the permission from connections that already have it.
- Deciding approvals in a Claude batch also needs the connection's permission to decide. A connection set so that decisions need your tap in AgentDash cannot prepare approval batches; decide those in AgentDash. Hire approvals are always left out of Claude batches.
- Limits: each person may have 5 batch previews open and confirm up to 1,000 records an hour. A Claude connection may also prepare up to 10 batches and confirm up to 200 records an hour.
- Creating, editing and deleting ceiling templates, and choosing the ceiling for new agents, are for owners and admins. Applying a template to agents needs the permission to manage agents.

## Upgrade

- Four migrations run on upgrade, `0158` to `0161`: `0158` adds the batch tables; `0159` adds the ceiling templates table and five company columns (the three switches, the records-per-batch number and the ceiling for new agents); `0160` adds a column that lets a routine be deleted while its runs stay, and one column to the batch table; `0161` adds two indexes to the activity log.
- `0161` builds its indexes with a plain `CREATE INDEX`, so writes to the activity log, and with them most writes in the product, wait while each index builds. On a large installation, build both first in a quiet window with `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY IF NOT EXISTS "activity_log_company_actor_created_idx" ON "activity_log" ("company_id", "actor_id", "created_at");` and `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY IF NOT EXISTS "activity_log_company_action_created_idx" ON "activity_log" ("company_id", "action", "created_at");`. The migration then does nothing.
- Existing Claude connections keep working as before. To prepare batch changes from Claude, connect again (see For admins).
- Rolling back to v2026.1109.1 restores the earlier behavior. The new tables and columns stay in place, and a routine deleted on this release shows again as an archived routine, with its triggers still off.

## Security

- A batch adds no authority. Each record is sent on its own, as the person who confirmed the batch, through the same check a single change goes through, so a batch cannot do what a single change would refuse. The person's access is read again before every record; for a batch from Claude, so are the connection, the person's owner or admin role and the company switch.
- A batch from Claude is confirmed only with a code shown on an AgentDash page in the admin's own browser. The code is stored only as a hash, works once for one batch, and five wrong tries cancel the batch. Text in records that looks like a code is masked in what the Claude tools pass along.
- A Claude connection cannot read the admin audit, the list of agents a member looks after, agent memory, the owner and admin inbox view or two evaluation reports, and the admin read switches never apply to it.
- The earlier gap that let any member make company-level decisions was closed in v2026.1109.1. This release does not change that.

## Breaking Changes

- Assistant connections (OAuth grants) are refused on: agent memory, `GET /api/companies/:companyId/agents/:agentId/memory` and `/memory/history` (404); the inbox override view, `GET /api/companies/:companyId/inbox/override` (403); and `GET /api/companies/:companyId/evaluation/replay` and `/evaluation/shadow-report` (403).

## API changes

- Admin-switched reads: `GET /api/companies/:companyId/agents/:agentId/directives` and the run detail routes `GET /api/heartbeat-runs/:runId`, `/events` and `/log` accept an owner or admin when the matching company switch is on, never through an assistant connection; every such read is logged (transcript reads once per person and run per 10 minutes).
- New routes: `GET /api/companies/:companyId/audit` and `/audit.csv` (owners and admins; refused for assistant connections), `DELETE /api/routines/:id`, `PATCH /api/labels/:labelId`, the ceiling template routes under `/api/companies/:companyId/ceiling-templates`, and `GET /api/companies/:companyId/members/:memberId/stewarded-agents` (owners and admins; refused for assistant connections).
- `PATCH /api/companies/:companyId` accepts `adminDirectiveReadEnabled`, `adminTranscriptReadEnabled`, `assistantAdminBatchesEnabled`, `assistantBatchMaxRecords` (1 to 200) and `defaultCeilingTemplateId` (a template of that company, or null).
- `POST /api/companies/:companyId/members/:memberId/archive` accepts `stewardTransfers`.
- New OAuth scope `agentdash:admin`, listed in `scopes_supported`. A call that needs it from a grant without it gets 403 `insufficient_scope` with a `WWW-Authenticate` step-up challenge. The consent screen never pre-checks it, and the server drops it from the grant unless the person is an owner or admin of the company and the company switch is on.
- New assistant MCP tools `prepare_batch_change`, `confirm_batch_change` and `batch_change_status`, served only to a grant that explicitly holds `agentdash:admin`.
- The batch routes (`/api/companies/:companyId/batches/...` and `/api/companies/:companyId/assistant/batches/...`) are internal and not part of the public contract yet.
