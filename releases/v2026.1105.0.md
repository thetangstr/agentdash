## Evaluation feature and limits

The native evaluation workflow and its evidence limits are those documented in [v2026.1104.0](https://github.com/thetangstr/agentdash/releases/tag/v2026.1104.0): explicit criteria, persisted-output evidence and separate execution, acceptance, project progress and business-outcome records. Retained deterministic evaluation evidence is bounded technical proof; it does not establish human calibration, semantic quality or customer business outcomes.

## Upgrade guide

No additional migrations relative to v2026.1104.0. Instances older than v2026.1031.0 must still follow that release's compatibility and Microsoft sign-in migration guidance.
